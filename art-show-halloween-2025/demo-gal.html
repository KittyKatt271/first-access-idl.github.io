The Black Cat

Created by: Katt Woodworth

Graphic Design & Web Development
Cohort: 6027

About the Artist

Katt Woodworth is an aspiring graphic and web designer, with a passion of storytelling and atmosphere. Drawing inspiration from horror, gothic, and fantasy themes, her work explores the emotional depth and haunting beauty found within the darkness.

About the Piece

Inspired by Edgar Allan Poe's “The Black Cat,” this piece captures the story's descent into madness, where guilt festers into blinding rage and echoes a mind unraveling beneath the weight of its own deeds. Through the interplay of shadow and flame, it reflects key elements of the tale, providing a visual representation of the narrative.

No One Home

Created by: Mel Long

Recording Arts & Show Production
Cohort: 351

About the Artist

Once an aspiring book editor, Mel Long is following a new dream to become an audio engineer. She had no idea what to expect when she began her journey at F.I.R.S.T. Institute, and now she is thrilled to be here. No One Home is one of the six songs she made in 101 and her classmates' favorite.

About the Piece

Have you ever had a nightmare that you were left all alone in the world? You know that feeling of dread and loneliness that washes over you? That is what No One Home is meant to encompass: those moments when you feel lost and alone and don't know what to do ... because you never imagined there'd be a time when no one was there for you.
