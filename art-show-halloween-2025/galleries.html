Crossroads at Twilight

Created by: Chloe Kistler

Graphic Design & Web Development
Cohort: 6027

About the Artist

My name is Chloe Kistler, and I am a Graphic Design and Web Development Student here at F.I.R.S.T. . My passion for Graphic Design started fast and the need to learn more has grown even faster. I love finding creative ways to make visuals that connect with people. Whether it is with layouts, colors, or typography- I draw inspiration through everyday life, music and early 2000’s aesthetics. Graphic design has become my favorite way of using my creativity, and I don’t expect it to stop anytime soon!

About the Piece

This image radiates that perfect early-2000s Halloween nostalgia — like something pulled straight from a Goosebumps cover, or the background of a Flash game on AddictingGames.com.

The giant orange moon overlooks the sky, giving off those moody gradients, the witch on her broomstick feels like she’s about to glide across your old monitor, chased by vector bats that look like they were cut from construction paper. The purple-to-orange sky gradient screams spooky desktop wallpaper circa 2004, while the silhouetted trees bring back memories of Halloween clip art and pumpkin-carving stencils you’d print.

Even the crooked wooden signs pointing to “Black Cat Tavern,” “Art Show,” and “Ghostly Graves” feel like they’d belong in an early Club Penguin Halloween Party. It’s got that comforting mix of eerie and playful—the kind of digital eeriness that lived in the era of Haunted Mansion screensavers, creepy MIDI soundtracks, and flashlight games on Miniclip.

Born and raised in Houston, Texas, I’m an audio engineer and sound artist with a lifelong devotion to the power of music.
